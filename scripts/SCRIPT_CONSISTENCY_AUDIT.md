# Virtual Screening Workflow Consistency

The public script has been updated to match the final virtual-screening workflow.

The retained workflow includes:

- generation of 50,000 virtual nanoparticle candidates;
- continuous-variable sampling according to the finalized candidate-generation strategy;
- material-aware categorical generation;
- Type-MAT-Shape joint sampling based on observed real-database combinations;
- chemistry-aware feasibility filtering;
- numerical feasibility filtering;
- out-of-distribution filtering;
- CatBoost high-delivery probability scoring;
- top-candidate ranking;
- cancer-type-specific candidate screening;
- local working-range estimation.

The final public workflow no longer independently samples Type, MAT, and Shape categories.

Instead, Type-MAT-Shape combinations are sampled jointly from observed database combinations to maintain chemical feasibility.

The generation metadata records:

- requested candidate number;
- deduplicated candidate number;
- final filtered candidate number;
- Type-MAT-Shape sampling strategy;
- applied chemistry-aware constraints.
